# Surface brief: lobby and landing (src/pages/index.tsx)

Scope: the signed-out landing and the signed-in lobby are one route. Visitor mode: Persuade. The in-game screen (GameView) inherits this world as an Operate surface.

Audience and job: a judge or a chess player with under a minute. Understand that each piece carries money, then start, join or watch a game.
Action: Play (one passkey prompt, optional username). Signed in: start a board, sit down at an open one, or watch a live one.
Proof on hand: the live 3D board playing a real scripted game; real boards from the chain; measured sign-up and move times.
Constraints: testnet only; no invented numbers; excellent on phones; never busy; no template layout.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The pieces are alive because each one is worth something. The page is a living board, not a pitch about one. It refuses the web3 landing arrangement of a centred hero over rows of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: A drenched indigo field. Bone and obsidian are the two sides and the only solid surfaces. Gold is light, and light means money: it appears only as the core inside a piece, an amount, or the action that stakes. Everything is built on the board's square module: controls are squares of a rank, the selected one turns gold. Living things have eyes that follow you and a core that beats. No glass, no glowing chrome, no gradient type. One characterful grotesque for everything.

STORY: Sees pieces watching them. Sees a capture move a core from one piece to another. Understands a piece's worth by touching it. Presses Play.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed 3D board, low and close, pieces looking at the pointer. Flush-left at the bottom: the statement in large condensed bone type, one sentence under it, then a rank of squares holding the username field and a gold Play square. One piece carries a tethered tag with its worth. Nothing else.

FORM: A direction pinned by the owner's brief after two re-rolls ("chess in a web3 dimension; the pieces are alive and all valuable"), replacing assigned candidate 7 of the grounded list. Seed key 36bc30a9. Signature interaction: the core transfer on capture. The lobby is the Yard: every game is a small living board showing its real position.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Memorable moment: a piece is taken and its glowing core flies into the piece that took it.
Unresolved: whether sides keep any names beyond White and Black.
